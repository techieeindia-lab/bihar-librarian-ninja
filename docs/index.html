Privacy Policy

App: Bihar Librarian Ninja (Bihar School Librarian Exam Prep 2026)

🔒 100% Privacy & Zero Tracking Commitment

Bihar Librarian Ninja does NOT collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal information. You do not need to register or log in. All your test progress, daily streaks, mistake records, and bookmarks stay 100% privately on your personal device.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Bihar Librarian Ninja. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning the confidentiality and protection of information when you use our educational Android application designed for candidates appearing in the Bihar School Librarian Examination (BSEB LET & BPSC).

2. Personal Data We Do NOT Collect

Because our application is architected around privacy and offline utility, we do not collect:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): No names, email addresses, mobile phone numbers, or government identification IDs.
  • No Authentication Credentials: No logins, passwords, OTPs, or social media profiles.
  • No Location Data: We do not ask for or collect your GPS or network location coordinates.
  • No Financial Information: No bank accounts, UPI IDs, credit/debit card numbers, or transaction histories.
  • No Sensitive Device Permissions: Our app requires zero sensitive Android permissions. We do not access your camera, contacts, photos, microphone, or external storage.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide a functional offline learning experience, the app saves your study progress exclusively inside your device's private sandbox using AsyncStorage:

  • Quiz Records & Accuracy: Your completed test scores, total questions answered, and percentage accuracy.
  • Saved Questions: Questions you manually choose to bookmark for revision.
  • Mistake Notebook: Questions answered incorrectly during quizzes so you can practice them again.
  • Daily Streak: Your consecutive days of study calculated from your local device clock.
  • App Settings: Language selection (Hindi/English) and Theme mode (Dark/Light).

Note: This data is never sent to our servers. If you clear the app data or uninstall the app, this local study history is permanently removed.

4. Cloud Connections (Read-Only Content Delivery)

When an internet connection is available, the app connects to a secure cloud database (Supabase) solely to download educational syllabus updates (e.g., newly added practice questions or updated one-liners). No user data, device telemetry, or personal identity is sent during these read-only updates.

5. Data Retention & Data Deletion Policy

Because Bihar Librarian Ninja does not require user accounts or collect personally identifiable information (PII), we do not store, retain, or process your personal data on external servers. All study metrics, bookmarks, and quiz attempts remain strictly inside your device's private sandbox storage.

How to Delete Your Data: You have full control over your data. You can permanently wipe all locally saved data and settings at any time by:

  • Going to your Android device Settings > Apps > Bihar Librarian Ninja > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data, or
  • Uninstalling the application from your device.

6. Advertising & Third-Party Trackers

Bihar Librarian Ninja contains zero third-party advertising SDKs (no Google AdMob, Unity, AppLovin, or Facebook Audience Network). We do not perform cross-app tracking or user profiling.

7. Children's Privacy

This application is intended for adult students and university graduates holding a BLIS/MLIS degree preparing for the Bihar School Librarian competitive recruitment. It is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.
